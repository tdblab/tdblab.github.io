Experimental platform + instrument

RAMFISH

Rapid Amplified Multiplexed FISH

An automated machine for multiplexed FISH. It handles the liquids, keeps the temperature right, and uses live imaging to time each staining round.

  • Detects many mRNAs in one tissue
  • Affordable, accessible and easy to use
  • Faster, automated protocol

Instrument

NanoPulse Microinjector

Precise liquid delivery for CRISPR

A custom-built injector that pushes out tiny, exact volumes, from nanoliters to microliters, using a small pump driven by a fine-step motor.

  • About 0.03 µL per pulse
  • Programmable injection settings
  • Modular pump design
  • Made for embryos and tissue

Robotics + vision

FISHER_α

Flexible Instrument for Stimulus-controlled Habituation and Experimental Reinforcement

An open-source automatic fish feeder for behavior studies. It pairs each feeding with a light cue, so researchers can study learning, habits and drug effects.

  • Light cues paired with feeding
  • Battery powered, runs unattended
  • Counts every trial

Bio-automation

CORN-DRIVE

Closed-loop Observation and Robotic Nurture platform

A robotic platform that watches corn plants grow, senses their environment, and adjusts care automatically over time.

  • Cameras and AI spot problems early
  • Environmental sensing
  • Adjusts water and nutrients on its own
  • Tuned for corn plants
